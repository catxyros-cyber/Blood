Last updated: [DATE]. This policy explains what [YOUR COMPANY NAME] ("we") collects when you use the MOTION launcher, the MOTION website and MOTION Premium.

On your PC (the launcher)

  • Settings, installations, mods and worlds are stored on your computer. We don't receive them.
  • Microsoft and Minecraft sign-in tokens are stored in the Windows Credential Manager on your PC. They are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang — never to us. We never see your password.
  • The launcher contacts Mojang, Microsoft, Modrinth and mod-loader websites to download what you ask for. Those services receive your IP address under their own privacy policies.

MOTION account and Premium

  • When you sign in to MOTION's online features, Mojang confirms your Minecraft profile. We store your Minecraft UUID and username, when you created your MOTION account, and session records (device label, creation and last-use time).
  • If you buy Premium, payments are processed by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID and the status of your subscription or purchase — not your card details.
  • With cloud sync (Premium), we store the launcher settings and installation metadata you choose to sync (names, versions, loaders, memory settings). Never mods, worlds or tokens.
  • Our servers keep short-lived technical logs (time, request path, status, IP address) for security and troubleshooting, retained for [30] days.
  • The account page shows your skin's face using mc-heads.net, which receives your Minecraft UUID.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no selling of data, no third-party analytics or trackers in the launcher or on this website.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your MOTION account from the account page at any time; this removes your account, sessions and synced data. Payment records are kept by Stripe as required by law.
  • Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].
