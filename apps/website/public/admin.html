Capes

Publish capes for MOTION players. Use Minecraft's cape layout: 64×32, or HD up to 2048×1024 (recommended for still capes; 1024×512 for animated ones). Any other picture (a wallpaper, a 4K artwork) is wrapped onto an HD cape for you. PNG, JPG, WebP or an animated GIF (up to 64 frames); for animations you can also upload a PNG strip with the frames stacked top to bottom. Only players using MOTION see these capes.

Accessories

Wings and auras for MOTION players. Players wear one of each, pick them in the launcher (Customize), and only MOTION players see them. Wings: upload a PNG of one wing with a transparent background, the shoulder about halfway down the left edge and the tip on the right (the other wing is mirrored); any size up to 2048 px. They rest folded, breathe when you stand still and spread and flap as you move. Auras: a colour and a particle style.

Community skins

Publish a 64×64 Minecraft skin to the MOTION community catalog. Every MOTION user can preview it in My Skin and add it to their local library. Applying it changes that player's Minecraft account skin, visible to all Minecraft players.

Tags

Give a player a tag shown before their name in game, like [OWNER]. Only MOTION players see tags. The player must have signed in to the MOTION launcher once. Leave the tag empty to remove it.

All keys

Keys are listed by their last five characters. Reset PC lets the key be activated on any PC again (for a player who can't wait 24 hours). Disable stops it working everywhere.
